Penafian (Disclaimer)

Last updated: August 7, 2026 Terakhir diperbarui: 7 Agustus 2026

Nias Heritage (Ondröita Nono Niha) is a curated digital application designed to provide access to historical documentation, oral traditions, proverbs (Amaedola), traditional songs (Sinunö), epic poetry (Hoho), folklores (Manö-manö), and literature regarding Nias Island (Tanö Niha), Indonesia.

1. Content Sources

  • Textual Content: All textual materials displayed in the app (including proverbs, dictionary entries, articles, and scriptures) are sourced directly from publicly accessible open projects such as Wikimedia Incubator (Nias Wikibooks) and public domain cultural records.
  • Images & Media: Images displayed within the application are sourced from Wikimedia Commons under their respective Creative Commons licenses, or from verified open digital archives.

2. Accuracy of Information

  • Nias Heritage aggregates and formats content for digital preservation and accessibility. The app developers do not alter, forge, or verify the historical or linguistic correctness of original wiki articles. The responsibility for accuracy lies solely with the original wiki writers and community contributors.
  • Users are encouraged to consult primary academic literature and community elders for historical or research verification.

3. Intellectual Property

  • All rights to texts and images remain with their respective original authors and copyright holders under Creative Commons and open-content licenses.
  • Nias Heritage does not claim ownership of any historical texts or community-submitted wiki entries displayed in the app.

4. Non-Profit & Educational Usage

  • All content provided through Nias Heritage is offered free of charge for non-profit cultural preservation, educational, and linguistic research purposes.

5. Limitation of Liability

  • The developers of Nias Heritage shall not be held liable for any inaccuracies, typographical errors, omissions, or damages resulting from the use or inability to use the information provided in the app.

6. Contact Us

Warisan Nias (Ondröita Nono Niha) adalah aplikasi digital terkurasi yang dirancang untuk menyediakan akses terhadap dokumentasi sejarah, tradisi lisan, pepatah (Amaedola), nyanyian tradisional (Sinunö), puisi epik (Hoho), cerita rakyat (Manö-manö), dan literatur budaya Nias (Tanö Niha), Indonesia.

1. Sumber Konten
